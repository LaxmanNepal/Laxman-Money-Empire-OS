# Monetization Matrix

| Asset | Audience | Fastest realistic revenue | Scalable revenue | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---:|
| YouTube | Nepali tech/AI viewers | Services, affiliate, sponsors | YPP, memberships, products | 10/10 |
| Facebook | Broad Nepali audience | Leads, sponsors, affiliate | Platform monetization | 9/10 |
| Nisulka Tools | Search users | Affiliate/tool partnerships | Ads + premium tools | 9/10 |
| NEPSE | Investors | Premium reports/services | Subscription intelligence | 9/10 |
| Nepali-Patro | Daily utility users | Ads when traffic qualifies | Ads + premium utilities | 8/10 |
| Digital products | Learners/creators | Direct sales | Product catalogue | 9/10 |
| B2B automation | SMEs/creators | Service fees | Retainers/productized SaaS | 9/10 |
| Games | Casual users | Ads | Large traffic asset | 5/10 |
| Live TV | Viewers | Ads where lawful | Traffic asset | 4/10 |

## Revenue stack

### Advertising
Useful at scale, but low control and variable RPM.

### Affiliate
Best for software/tool tutorials where the recommendation is genuinely useful.

### Sponsorship
Sell a clearly defined package: integration, Short/Reel, Facebook post, website placement, newsletter, etc.

### Services
Fastest path to cash if you can sell a result. Examples:

- Website setup
- SEO audit
- AI workflow setup
- YouTube channel setup
- Thumbnail packages
- Social content systems
- Business automation

### Digital products
Create once, improve repeatedly:

- Excel templates
- Prompt packs
- Creator kits
- Website templates
- Nepali-language guides
- Automation templates
- Business calculators

### SaaS/subscription
Only after proving users will pay. Never build a large SaaS product first and search for customers later.

## Revenue rule

For every product define:

`Customer → Pain → Offer → Price → Acquisition → Conversion → Delivery → Retention`

If any one of these is unknown, the idea is not ready for serious development.
