# Laxman Nepal Digital Empire — Business Model

## The core thesis

Do not build a collection of unrelated websites. Build a network where each asset reinforces the others.

## Business lines

### 1. Media

YouTube, Facebook, short-form content, articles.

Revenue: platform monetization, sponsors, affiliate offers.

### 2. Utility software

Nisulka Tools and future practical web applications.

Revenue: advertising, premium features, sponsorships, B2B tools.

### 3. Information products

Nepali calendar, gold prices, exchange rates, useful datasets, educational information.

Revenue: advertising, sponsorships, premium features.

### 4. Financial intelligence

NEPSE analytics and educational tools.

Revenue: premium analytics, subscriptions, sponsorships where compliant.

### 5. Digital products

Templates, guides, calculators, creator resources, software resources.

Revenue: direct sales.

### 6. Services

Website creation, automation, AI workflow setup, SEO audits, content systems.

Revenue: direct client fees.

## The flywheel

Content creates discovery.

Discovery creates users.

Users create data.

Data reveals problems.

Problems become products.

Products create revenue.

Revenue funds better products/content.

## What not to do

- Launch dozens of unrelated sites.
- Build features without traffic.
- Depend exclusively on AdSense.
- Copy competitors without differentiation.
- Sell financial certainty.
- Use spammy AI content.
- Assume automation removes the need for judgment.

## Empire criteria

An asset deserves continued investment when it has at least two of:

- recurring traffic
- recurring revenue
- strong search demand
- proprietary data
- audience ownership
- repeat customers
- automation advantage
- defensible brand trust

## Long-term objective

Build a portfolio where the creator's time increasingly shifts from repetitive production to:

- strategy
- partnerships
- product decisions
- high-value content
- audience relationships
- capital allocation
