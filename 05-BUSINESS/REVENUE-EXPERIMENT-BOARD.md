## Priority experiments for Laxman

### 1. Nepali creator tool affiliate content
- Content: practical demonstrations of genuinely useful creator/AI tools.
- Distribution: YouTube + Facebook + website.
- Revenue: legitimate affiliate programs where available.
- Test: 3 videos + one comparison page.

### 2. Digital creator template pack
- Product: thumbnail, content planner, script and publishing templates.
- Customer: Nepali creators and small businesses.
- Test: landing page + sample + direct outreach.

### 3. Website/automation service
- Customer: Nepal small businesses and creators.
- Offer: simple website + automation + analytics package.
- Test: create one demo and contact 20 qualified prospects.

### 4. Nisulka Tools traffic-to-revenue
- Build high-demand utility pages.
- Improve SEO/internal linking.
- Add appropriate monetization only after useful traffic exists.

### 5. NEPSE data product
- Start with educational dashboards/data summaries.
- Validate willingness to pay before building a complex subscription.
- Never present AI output as guaranteed investment advice.

### 6. YouTube sponsorship pipeline
- Build a one-page media kit.
- Track audience, topics and demonstrated expertise.
- Contact relevant brands only after audience/fit evidence exists.

## Rule

Do not run more than three active revenue experiments at once. An idea is not a business until a real person demonstrates willingness to pay or a repeatable monetization mechanism is proven.
