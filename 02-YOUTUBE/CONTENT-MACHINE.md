# YouTube Content Machine

## Objective

Create a repeatable system that turns one strong idea into multiple useful assets without becoming repetitive AI spam.

## The 7-stage machine

### 1. Discover

Sources:

- YouTube analytics
- Search suggestions
- Competitor videos
- Audience comments
- Google trends/search demand
- Product/tool launches
- Nepal-specific problems
- Questions repeatedly asked by viewers

### 2. Score

Every idea gets a /100 score based on:

```text
Demand                 15
Nepali relevance       15
Viewer payoff          15
Packaging potential    10
Search potential       10
Browse potential       10
Monetization           10
Evergreen value         5
Production feasibility  5
Trust risk              5
```

### 3. Produce

Long-form structure:

```text
Hook
 ↓
Problem
 ↓
Promise
 ↓
Demonstration
 ↓
Explanation
 ↓
Practical examples
 ↓
Limitations
 ↓
Best use case
 ↓
CTA
```

### 4. Package

Generate multiple title options and thumbnail concepts. Select based on clarity and truthful curiosity, not clickbait.

### 5. Publish

Maintain consistent metadata, chapters where useful, descriptions, playlists, and relevant internal links.

### 6. Repurpose

From one long-form video, create only genuinely useful derivatives:

- 2–5 Shorts
- 1–3 Facebook posts/Reels
- 1 website article when there is search value
- 1 community post when appropriate
- FAQ snippets

### 7. Measure

Record performance at 24h, 72h, 7d, and 28d.

## Content categories

### A — High priority

Practical AI and technology problems with obvious viewer outcomes.

### B — Growth

Trending technology and tools with a Nepal-specific angle.

### C — Evergreen

Tutorials that can generate search traffic for months.

### D — Authority

Deep explanations, comparisons, experiments, and case studies.

### E — Monetization

Topics naturally connected to legitimate products, software, services, affiliates, or your own tools.

## Content calendar rule

Never fill the calendar simply to hit a number. A strong idea deserves more production effort than a weak idea.

## Repurposing rule

The derivative must provide a distinct value. Do not simply cut the same sentence into five near-identical posts.

## AI quality gate

Before publishing AI-assisted content, verify:

- Facts
- Product claims
- Pricing
- URLs
- Screenshots
- Dates
- Copyright/licensing
- Nepali language quality
- Originality
- Real viewer usefulness
