# Laxman Nepal Official — Nepali Audience Content Strategy

## Positioning

**A practical Nepali technology + AI + digital skills channel for people who want to use technology to work, learn, create and earn.**

Do not become a generic "AI news" channel. The differentiator is useful implementation in Nepali.

## Primary audience

1. Nepali students and young workers.
2. Nepali creators and small businesses.
3. Nepali users trying to use AI and software productively.
4. Nepali diaspora who need Nepal-specific digital information.

## Content pillars

| Pillar | Purpose | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| AI that solves a problem | Reach | Free AI tools, automation, image/video workflows |
| Practical computer skills | Search | Windows, Photoshop, Office, troubleshooting |
| Creator tools | Commercial intent | YouTube, thumbnails, editing, SEO, automation |
| Online work | High intent | Freelancing tools, productivity, digital services |
| Nepal digital life | Local authority | Nepali apps, services, websites, digital processes |
| Finance/NEPSE technology | High-value niche | Data tools, dashboards, analysis education |

## Content ratio

Start with approximately:

- 40% searchable evergreen tutorials
- 25% timely AI/tool opportunities
- 15% creator/YouTube workflows
- 10% Nepal-specific digital topics
- 10% experiments

Rebalance using analytics rather than opinion.

## Video formula

### Long-form

1. First 5–15 seconds: state the problem and payoff.
2. Show the result early.
3. Explain only what the viewer needs.
4. Demonstrate the workflow.
5. Include mistakes/limitations.
6. Give a practical next step.
7. CTA should be relevant, not begging for subscriptions.

### Shorts

- One problem.
- One surprising result.
- One demonstration.
- One takeaway.
- Optional bridge to a deeper video/tool.

## Topic scoring

For every idea calculate:

`Search demand + viewer pain + novelty + commercial intent + fit with channel + ability to demonstrate - competition`

Use a 100-point score.

## Title principles

Prefer specific outcomes:

- "यो AI ले 10 मिनेटको काम 30 सेकेन्डमा गर्छ"
- "फोटोबाट Background हटाउने सबैभन्दा सजिलो तरिका"
- "नेपालमा काम लाग्ने 7 Free AI Tools"

Avoid fake urgency, fake income claims and misleading promises.

## Thumbnail principles

- One main idea.
- 2–5 large words maximum where possible.
- Strong visual contrast.
- Human emotion only when it adds meaning.
- Do not duplicate the title word-for-word.
- Test concept before polishing design.

## Series strategy

Build recognizable series:

- `AI नेपालीमा`
- `Free Tool Friday`
- `1 Minute Tech`
- `Creator Lab`
- `Nepal Digital Guide`

Series make production easier and give viewers a reason to return.

## Repurposing

One strong long-form video should create:

`1 long video → 3–7 Shorts → 3–5 Facebook posts/reels → 1 website article → 1 tool/resource page → 1 community post`

Do not copy the same text everywhere. Adapt the hook to each platform.

## Weekly cadence

Baseline target for a solo creator:

- 2 strong long-form videos/week
- 4–7 Shorts/week
- 5–10 Facebook-native posts/reels/week
- 1 analytics review/week

Increase only when quality remains stable.
