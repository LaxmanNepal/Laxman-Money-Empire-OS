# YouTube Monetization & AI Content Checklist

**Last verified:** 2026-08-25

## Current full YPP path

YouTube currently documents two full ad-revenue entry paths:

- 1,000 subscribers + 4,000 qualified public watch hours in the previous 12 months; or
- 1,000 subscribers + 10 million qualified public Shorts views in the previous 90 days.

Shorts Feed watch hours do not count toward the 4,000-hour threshold. Eligibility is also subject to policies, account requirements and channel review.

## Expanded YPP

Where available, an earlier YPP tier can provide selected fan-funding/shopping features at 500 subscribers plus 3 public uploads in 90 days and either 3,000 qualified long-form watch hours in 365 days or 3 million qualified Shorts views in 90 days. Always verify availability for the channel's country and the current official requirements.

## Originality rules

YouTube's current monetization policy emphasizes original/authentic content and identifies repetitive or mass-produced content as inauthentic. Reused material must contain meaningful original commentary, modification or educational/entertainment value. Copyright remains a separate issue.

### Safe automation principle

Automate:
- research organization
- transcription
- draft generation
- metadata drafts
- clipping suggestions
- file management
- publishing checklists
- analytics collection
- reporting

Do not automate away:
- factual verification
- original perspective
- demonstrations
- editorial judgment
- meaningful commentary
- audience understanding

## Pre-publication gate

- [ ] Topic is useful to the target audience.
- [ ] Facts are verified.
- [ ] Any sponsored/affiliate relationship is handled correctly.
- [ ] Visual/audio assets are licensed or original.
- [ ] The video has meaningful original contribution.
- [ ] It is not a near-copy of previous uploads.
- [ ] AI use does not create misleading realism without required disclosure.
- [ ] Title accurately represents the video.
- [ ] Thumbnail accurately represents the video.
- [ ] No fabricated earnings claims.
- [ ] No fake testimonials.
- [ ] Financial claims are clearly educational and evidence-based.

## Policy source

Use the official YouTube Help Center as the source of truth and re-check before major monetization decisions.
