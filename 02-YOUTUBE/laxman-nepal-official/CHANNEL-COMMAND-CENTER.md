## Packaging rules

### Title

Promise a specific outcome. Avoid generic titles such as "Top 10 AI Tools" unless the list has a compelling angle.

### Thumbnail

One visual idea. Very few words. Strong contrast. No unnecessary UI clutter. The thumbnail should create curiosity without lying.

### First 30 seconds

Do not begin with a long greeting or generic introduction. Show the problem, result, or payoff quickly.

## Post-publish checkpoints

### 24 hours

Check early impressions, CTR, views, comments, and obvious packaging problems.

### 72 hours

Evaluate whether the video is receiving sustained distribution.

### 7 days

Compare retention and subscriber conversion against similar videos.

### 28 days

Classify:

- Winner
- Useful evergreen
- Slow burner
- Failed experiment

## Decision rules

If a topic repeatedly wins, create a series.

If a topic gets impressions but few clicks, improve packaging.

If clicks are strong but retention is weak, improve the opening/content delivery.

If views are strong but subscriber conversion is weak, improve channel positioning and CTA/context.

If a topic consistently fails, stop making it unless there is a strategic reason.
