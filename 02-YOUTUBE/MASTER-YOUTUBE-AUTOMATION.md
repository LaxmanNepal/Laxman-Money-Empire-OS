# Master YouTube Automation

## Principle

Automate administration and transformation, not authenticity.

The channel should remain human-led where trust matters: topic judgment, final fact check, personal demonstrations, final script approval and final upload review.

## Pipeline

```text
Trend / Search / Analytics
        ↓
Opportunity database
        ↓
Topic score
        ↓
Brief
        ↓
Research
        ↓
Fact verification
        ↓
Script
        ↓
Recording / screen capture / visuals
        ↓
Edit
        ↓
Thumbnail + title variants
        ↓
Upload
        ↓
Repurpose
        ↓
Analytics
        ↓
Learning
```

## Automation candidates

### Safe to automate heavily
- Topic database maintenance.
- Content calendar.
- File naming.
- Script formatting.
- Description templates.
- Chapter formatting.
- SEO checklist.
- Repurposing drafts.
- Thumbnail brief generation.
- Performance reports.
- Publishing queue reminders.

### Keep human review
- News claims.
- Financial claims.
- Copyright/licensing decisions.
- Sponsorship claims.
- Final title/thumbnail.
- Final script.
- Brand voice.

## Content database fields

`id, topic, pillar, audience, intent, score, source_urls, script_status, production_status, publish_date, video_url, views_24h, views_7d, subscribers_gained, watch_hours, lessons`

## Automation cadence

### Daily
- Pull latest channel snapshot.
- Check recent video performance.
- Identify winners/losers.
- Generate 5–10 topic candidates.
- Update content queue.

### Weekly
- Produce performance report.
- Re-rank content pillars.
- Select next week's long-form topics.
- Generate repurposing queue.
- Review monetization progress.

### Monthly
- Reposition if necessary.
- Archive failed experiments.
- Double down on proven topic clusters.

## Anti-spam rules

- Do not mass-produce near-identical videos.
- Do not scrape and read articles without original value.
- Do not create fake news for clicks.
- Do not fabricate screenshots or results.
- Do not use copyrighted media without permission.
- AI output must be reviewed and transformed into useful original work.

## Automation success metric

The goal is not `100% automation`.

The goal is:

**more quality output per hour of Laxman's time.**
