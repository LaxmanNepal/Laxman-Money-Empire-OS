# Empire Playbook

## Objective
Build a Nepal-focused digital business system where audience, software, data, content and products reinforce each other. The target is not one viral post; it is a portfolio of owned assets producing several revenue streams.

## The flywheel

`Research → Content → Distribution → Traffic → Trust → Product → Revenue → Data → Better Research`

## Revenue ladder

### Level 1 — attention
- YouTube long-form
- YouTube Shorts
- Facebook Reels/posts
- Search traffic
- Community distribution

### Level 2 — low-friction monetization
- YouTube YPP when eligible
- Website advertising when traffic and eligibility justify it
- Affiliate/referral commissions
- Sponsorships
- Creator services

### Level 3 — owned products
- Premium tools
- Digital templates
- Excel/Google Sheets products
- AI prompt packs
- Courses/workshops
- Website templates
- Creator resources

### Level 4 — recurring revenue
- SaaS subscriptions
- NEPSE premium intelligence
- Pro analytics dashboards
- Business automation subscriptions
- Membership/community

### Level 5 — B2B
- Website development
- Automation setup
- SEO systems
- Social content systems
- Data dashboards
- AI workflow implementation

## Asset portfolio

1. **Laxman Nepal Official** — authority + distribution.
2. **Nisulka Tools** — searchable utility portfolio + future premium tools.
3. **Nepali-Patro** — recurring daily traffic and utility ecosystem.
4. **NEPSE intelligence** — high-intent finance audience; accuracy first.
5. **Gold/data pages** — daily search demand and social content.
6. **Games** — retention and repeat visits, not the primary business.
7. **Live TV** — only where redistribution rights permit it.
8. **Digital products** — direct revenue independent of ad approval.

## Business rules

- Do not launch a new property merely because it is technically interesting.
- Prefer assets that can share users, data, content or distribution with existing assets.
- Every new project must have a target user, acquisition path and monetization path before coding.
- Build the smallest useful version first.
- Kill projects that show no meaningful traction after a defined experiment window.
- Never depend on one platform for all revenue.

## Opportunity scoring

Score every idea from 1–5 for:

`Demand + Monetization + Distribution Fit + Build Speed + Defensibility - Risk`

Prioritize high-demand, high-monetization, fast-to-launch ideas that fit the Nepali market.

## 90-day operating rule

Spend roughly 70% of execution on proven/high-signal assets, 20% on adjacent experiments and 10% on speculative experiments. Do not let experiments consume the core production schedule.
