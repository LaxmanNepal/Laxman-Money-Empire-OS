# August–November 2026 Execution Plan

## Hard objective
Pursue YouTube monetization while building at least one direct-revenue path so the business is not dependent on YPP approval.

## Phase 1 — Aug 25–31

### YouTube
- Lock 3 content pillars.
- Publish 2 strong long-form videos.
- Publish 4–7 Shorts derived from them.
- Build a backlog of 30 scored topics.
- Review the weakest 20% of recent uploads.

### Facebook
- Establish a repeatable Reels/post cadence.
- Convert every strong YouTube topic into native Facebook content.

### Owned assets
- Improve Nisulka Tools discovery and SEO.
- Keep Nepali-Patro useful and fast.
- Continue NEPSE data infrastructure only after data-source reliability is established.

### Revenue
- Choose one digital product to validate.
- Choose one affiliate/service offer that naturally fits the audience.

## September

Goal: establish a production machine.

- 8+ long-form videos/month if quality can be maintained.
- Consistent Shorts pipeline.
- Weekly analytics review.
- First product/service sales experiment.
- Build email/contact capture where appropriate and privacy-compliant.
- Publish useful search pages around proven topics.

## October

Goal: scale winners.

- Identify top 2 topic clusters.
- Increase production around them.
- Build 1 stronger lead magnet or product.
- Approach relevant sponsors/partners with a simple media kit.
- Improve website conversion paths.
- Automate reporting and recurring data tasks.

## November

Goal: convert traction into a business system.

- Push YouTube toward applicable YPP thresholds.
- Apply if all requirements are actually met.
- Expand the best direct-revenue offer.
- Build a repeatable sponsor/affiliate pipeline.
- Package proven workflows into services/products.
- Review which projects deserve continued investment.

## YouTube reality check

As of the current official YouTube documentation, full ad-revenue YPP eligibility requires 1,000 subscribers plus either 4,000 valid public watch hours in the previous 12 months or 10 million valid public Shorts views in the previous 90 days. ## Weekly allocation

If time is limited:

- 40% YouTube production
- 20% distribution/repurposing
- 15% owned websites/tools
- 15% monetization/sales
- 10% analytics/automation

## Kill list

Stop or postpone:

- Random new websites with no distribution plan.
- Features nobody requested.
- Low-value AI content farms.
- Projects that cannot explain their monetization path.
- Automation that saves minutes but creates hours of debugging.

## Success by November

A strong outcome is not merely a subscriber number. It is:

1. A materially stronger YouTube channel.
2. A repeatable content/distribution machine.
3. At least one validated direct-revenue offer.
4. A growing owned web ecosystem.
5. Automated analytics and reporting.
6. A clear path to recurring revenue.
