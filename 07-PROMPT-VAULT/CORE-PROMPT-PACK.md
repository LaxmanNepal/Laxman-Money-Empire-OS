# Core Prompt Pack

These prompts are designed for this repository and Laxman's Nepal-focused business.

## 1. CEO / Cofounder

```text
Act as my brutally honest AI cofounder and execution strategist. Context: I am Laxman Nepal, building a Nepal-focused digital business around YouTube, Facebook, websites, tools, data products and digital products. My objective is sustainable revenue, not vanity metrics. Analyze the problem, identify the highest-leverage move, challenge weak assumptions, estimate effort, define measurable success, then give me an execution checklist. Do not flatter me. If my idea is low-value, say so and propose a better alternative.
```

## 2. YouTube topic selector

```text
Act as a Nepali YouTube growth strategist. Generate 20 topic opportunities for Laxman Nepal Official. Audience: Nepali technology users, students, creators and workers. Prioritize search demand, viewer pain, novelty, demonstration potential, subscriber conversion and monetization potential. Score every idea /100 and explain the score. Separate evergreen, timely and experimental ideas. Do not invent trends or statistics.
```

## 3. Video script

```text
Write a high-retention Nepali YouTube script for this verified topic: [TOPIC]. Audience: [AUDIENCE]. Goal: [GOAL]. Start with a concrete hook, show the outcome early, demonstrate the process, explain limitations, avoid filler, and end with a relevant CTA. Mark every factual claim that needs verification. Do not fabricate features, prices or results.
```

## 4. Title laboratory

```text
Generate 20 YouTube titles for [TOPIC] for a Nepali audience. Create 5 search-first, 5 curiosity-first, 5 outcome-first and 5 hybrid titles. Score each for clarity, click potential, search intent and honesty. Avoid clickbait that the video cannot satisfy.
```

## 5. Thumbnail strategist

```text
Create 10 thumbnail concepts for [VIDEO]. Each must communicate one idea in under two seconds. Give: main visual, 2–5 word text option, composition, emotion if useful, contrast strategy and why it should work. Do not repeat the title exactly.
```

## 6. Content repurposer

```text
Turn this source video/article into platform-native content: 5 Shorts, 5 Facebook posts, 3 Facebook Reels concepts and 1 website article outline. Preserve facts. Add original value. Do not produce near-duplicate spam. Each platform needs a different hook.
```

## 7. Business idea judge

```text
Judge this business idea for Nepal: [IDEA]. Score demand, willingness to pay, competition, build complexity, distribution, defensibility, legal/platform risk and time-to-first-revenue. Estimate the smallest experiment that can validate demand within 7 days. Tell me what would make you kill the idea.
```

## 8. Daily CEO review

```text
Review today's metrics and tasks: [DATA]. Tell me what changed, what matters, what is noise, what failed, what worked, the single highest-leverage action tomorrow, and which task I should stop doing. Be brutally honest.
```

## 9. Codebase monetization audit

```text
Inspect this repository as a product strategist and senior engineer. Identify features that can create traffic, leads, subscriptions, affiliate revenue, advertising revenue or direct sales. Rank opportunities by revenue potential, effort and time-to-market. Then implement the highest-value safe improvements.
```

## 10. Research verification

```text
Research [TOPIC]. Separate facts, primary-source claims, secondary reporting, inference and uncertainty. Cite authoritative sources. Do not fill gaps with guesses. Flag anything time-sensitive that must be rechecked immediately before publication.
```

## 11. Conversion optimizer

```text
Audit this landing page for a Nepali audience. Identify the user problem, promise, proof, friction, CTA, trust gaps and monetization opportunities. Rewrite only where evidence supports the change. Give a measurable A/B test plan.
```

## 12. Automation architect

```text
Design an automation for [WORKFLOW]. First identify the manual steps, trigger, inputs, transformations, outputs, failure modes, secrets required, retry strategy, logging and human approval points. Prefer GitHub Actions/API/JSON when appropriate. Never place credentials in source control.
```
