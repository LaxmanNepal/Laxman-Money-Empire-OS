# Facebook Automation Playbook

## Objective
Use Facebook as a distribution and audience-acquisition engine, not as a warehouse for copied posts.

## Content mix

- 30% useful Nepali tech/AI tips
- 20% short demonstrations
- 15% local digital information
- 15% creator/earning education
- 10% website/tool promotion
- 10% experiments/community content

Adjust from page analytics.

## Daily machine

```text
Research
 ↓
Fact check
 ↓
Post/reel draft
 ↓
Native Facebook hook
 ↓
Visual
 ↓
CTA
 ↓
Publish/schedule
 ↓
Measure
```

## Repurpose correctly

A YouTube video can produce Facebook content, but do not simply paste the YouTube title and description. Facebook needs a standalone hook, fast context and native visual pacing.

## Monetization routes

1. Platform monetization when the page/account is eligible.
2. Sponsorships.
3. Affiliate/referral links.
4. Traffic to owned websites.
5. Leads for services.
6. Digital product sales.
7. Tool/product partnerships.

Platform eligibility and available monetization products can change; always verify current status inside Meta's professional/monetization dashboards before making a business forecast.

## Growth rules

- Create shareable utility, not engagement bait.
- Reply to useful comments.
- Turn recurring questions into future posts.
- Use Nepal-specific examples.
- Test hooks aggressively, but keep claims truthful.
- Track which posts generate profile visits and website clicks, not just reactions.

## Weekly scorecard

`Reach | 3-second/video views | average watch time | shares | saves | comments | profile visits | link clicks | followers gained | revenue`

## Automation boundary

Automate scheduling, formatting, content queues and reporting. Keep human review for sensitive news, political claims, financial claims, sponsorships and copyright.
