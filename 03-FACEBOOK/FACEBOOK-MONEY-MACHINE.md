# Facebook Money Machine

## Objective

Use Facebook for distribution, community growth, traffic, and eventually monetization rather than treating it as a second copy of YouTube.

## Content sources

1. Original YouTube videos
2. Short-form clips
3. Nepal technology tips
4. Useful AI discoveries
5. Website/tool updates
6. Gold/finance data where accurately sourced
7. Community questions
8. Evergreen educational posts

## Distribution system

```text
Research
 ↓
Original asset
 ↓
Facebook-native adaptation
 ↓
Post/Reel
 ↓
Engagement
 ↓
Website/YouTube when relevant
 ↓
Audience ownership
```

## Do not automate blindly

Avoid:

- Repetitive AI posts
- Copied news
- Fake engagement
- Misleading captions
- Copyrighted video reposting
- Engagement bait
- Mass posting irrelevant links

Automation should schedule, format, measure, and distribute approved content. Human judgment remains required for sensitive/current information.

## Nepal audience strategy

Prioritize:

- Nepali language
- Practical problems
- Strong visual demonstrations
- Local examples
- Simple explanations
- Timely Nepal-relevant technology

## Weekly operating model

- 3–5 educational posts
- 3–7 Reels depending on production capacity
- 1–2 deeper posts that establish authority
- Community replies every day where possible

These are starting ranges, not quotas.

## Monetization ladder

1. Audience growth
2. Website traffic
3. Affiliate recommendations
4. Sponsorships
5. Platform monetization where eligible
6. Digital products
7. Services

## Facebook experiment log

For each experiment track:

- Date
- Format
- Topic
- Hook
- Reach
- Views
- Watch time where available
- Shares
- Comments
- Follows
- Link clicks
- Revenue
- Lesson

## Golden rule

A Facebook post is successful when it produces a useful business outcome, not merely when it produces a large vanity number.
